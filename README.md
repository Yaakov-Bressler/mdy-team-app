# MDY Team App

The MDY Aleh Zayis team app: your amudim, the Amud Tracker, submitting work, the Guide look-ups, deadlines, graphics, feedback and contacts, on phone and computer.

**Open it:** https://yaakov-bressler.github.io/mdy-team-app/

## Installing it

- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Install app**.
- **Computer:** open it in Chrome or Edge and click the install icon at the right end of the address bar.

Sign in with your Aleh Zayis account. Updates arrive on their own.

## How it works

The app is a static page. It signs each person in with Microsoft and talks to the team SharePoint site through Microsoft Graph **as that person**, so it can only see or change what they already can on the site. No team data is stored in this repository or anywhere else.

- **Tracker:** reads and updates the Amud Tracker list (any team member can move a stage). At the top, as on the site's Workflow page, one bar per volume in production (`volumes` in `config.js`), with Final and Closed shown together as Final. The Meilah volume (Meilah, Kinnim, Tamid, Middos) runs on eight stages only, set in `workflows` in `config.js`: Open, Generated, Edited, Mevukar, Kramer, Edited 2, Proofread, Final. The move sheet, Submit and the bars offer only those for its amudim. Kerisos (28 Oct 2026) runs on one list for New and Old rows alike: Open, Generated, Edited, Reviewed, Mevukar, Kramer, Edited 2, Proofread, Final. Berachos (from its go-live, YB 3 Nov 2026) has its own volume bar and nine stages: Open, Generated, Edited, Mevukar, Final Edited, AI Checked, AI Proofread, Proofread, Final; its deadlines can also be "Final Edited (final editors)", and a Done when choice with no Berachos stage (Post Kramer, Edited 2) leaves Berachos rows out rather than counting them done, as the site's deadlines core 2026-11-03-01 does. Written, Run, Post Kramer, Mevukar 2, Edited 3 and Review 2 are gone from Kerisos and Closed is now Final; a row still on one of them stays there until someone moves it, and Next/Submit suggest the stage that replaced it (`retiredNext`).
  Since 2 Nov 2026, as on the site's Workflow page: each amud shows how long it has been in its stage (the tracker's StageSince column; amber after a week, red after two, `stale` in `config.js`). Moving an amud forward starts its clock; moving it back leaves it, and the next load sets it from the row's version history. **Mine** on the Tracker shows only your amudim. The volume bars stay bars only (YB, 2 Nov 2026: the top stays sleek). Each deadline card (My work, Deadlines) carries one pace line: *On track*, *Ahead* or *Behind* when every amud in it has reached its owner, otherwise *Waiting* or *N ready now* with how many are still at earlier stages, and how many a working day are needed. The move sheet opens the amud's latest file (the site's Open-Amud page) and its History.
- **My work** shows *On your desk* (amudim from your deadlines that have reached you, plus starred ones) and *Coming later* (still at earlier stages). An amud reaches you at the stage your work starts from: Edited for a Mevukar deadline, Kramer for an Edited 2 deadline (in R' Waldman's folder), otherwise the stage just before the deadline's. Once a deadline is 3 days away or less, or overdue, every amud on it still to do is on your desk. Overdue deadlines stay on the desk until they are done (2 Nov 2026). **Show** switches to anyone else's desk: everyone with a deadline on the list, including one with no due date (shown as *No due date*), and Mrs. Mayer, whose proofreading desk is read from the tracker as on the site's My Desk (an amud at Edited 2, or on Berachos Final Edited, AI Checked or AI Proofread, is on her desk once Claude's proofreading pass is in the masechta's Proofreading › Claude folder; 5 Nov 2026). You are recognised by your Aleh Zayis address or your personal one (`team` in `config.js`, the same table as the site's).
- **Submit:** uploads the file into the masechta's folder (Editors › your folder by default), named in house form, and moves the amud's stage.
- **Guide:** reads the Transliteration list, Definitions list, House Rulings and Style Guide live from Resources. Phrases and Headers use the same index as the site's Guide page (Site Assets › MDYLookup), including the English lines with no Hebrew before them (`lines-<masechta>.json`).
  Spelling, Phrases, Headers and Definitions also find other forms of what was typed (29 Oct 2026, `widen.js`, the same engine as the site's Guide): verb forms and plurals (says/said, bring/brought, korban/korbanos, kohen/kohanim), transliteration spellings (shemitah/shemittah, Yanai/Yannai), and in Hebrew prefixes, malei/chaser and endings (מצוה/מצות, בהמה/בהמות). They are listed after the exact results, marked *other form*; **Exact only** under the search box turns this off and is remembered on the device. Ask Chaim Levy's look-ups stay exact.
- **Deadlines, Feedback:** the site's Deadlines and Team Feedback lists. Feedback stays private to the person who sent it.
  A deadline puts amudim on its owner's *My work* by its Masechta, Amudim and Done when columns, read the same way as the site's tally (`Claude/Deadlines/deadlines-core.js`): a volume ("Meilah volume" = Meilah, Kinnim, Tamid, Middos) or a list of masechtos, `all`, ranges, `all except …`, and terms naming a masechta. Only amudim not yet at the deadline's stage are listed. A deadline is yours when your surname is in its Who column or its title.
  The Deadlines screen (YB, 6 Nov 2026: "cleaner, there's too much info") shows one short card per deadline: days, title, date (and the owner only if the title doesn't name him), one progress bar and one pace word. Tapping it opens the Details, the full pace line, the amudim still to do with their stages, and the Amudim / Done when columns. **By date** / **By masechta** (kept per device, `mdyDlSort`; the site's Deadlines page has the same switch): by masechta groups First-round editing, Content editing, Final editing (Edited 2, Final Edited, Final), then Proofreading and Other, each in Shas order (Berachos, Temurah, Kerisos, Meilah, Kinnim, Tamid, Middos), then the earlier step, then the first amud. A finished deadline drops off once its due date has passed, as on the site.
- **Graphics:** the masechta's Graphics stage folders and the pictures in them. In Needs Approval R' Shonek can **Approve** (moves to Ready for Designing) or **Reject**; in Ready for Review R' Muller can **Accept** (Ready to Go) or **Send back**. Both rejections move the picture to Rejected with the reason saved beside it; every move asks once more, can be undone, and nothing is deleted (`gfxReview`, `gfxAdmins` in `config.js`). Graphics.xlsx and the tracker's picture slots are left to the graphics tasks, as before.
- **Guide › Edits:** how the editors usually change a phrase, from `edits-index.json` in Site Assets › MDYLookup (Kerisos writers' drafts and Temurah first edits, each against the delivered Final), the same look-up as the site's Guide.
- **Recent uploads** (5 Nov 2026): the 15 newest amud files in the masechtos' stage folders (Writers, Editors, Mevakrim, Proofreading, Final), newest first, the same list as the site's Recent Uploads page (Workflow menu). Read through Microsoft Search as the signed-in person, by Created; Claude, Archive, Resources and Graphics are left out. Refreshes every minute while open; a new file can take a few minutes to show.
- **Contacts:** the site's own Contacts page.
- **Ask Chaim Levy** (formerly Ask Claude): the AI assistant, the same as the site's Ask Chaim Levy page (`SitePages/Ask-Chaim-Levy.aspx`), runs on Claude, for style, process, edits and corpus reports. Questions go to the team's relay (`askRelay` in `config.js`), which holds the Claude API key and lets in only people signed in with an Aleh Zayis account. The app runs Claude's searches itself, over the rulebooks, the corpus and the transliteration and definitions lists; nothing about people, the tracker or deadlines is sent. Its source texts (Claude/Ask Claude data) cover the Gemara or Mishnah, the commentary of record (Rashi; Tosafos on Meilah, the Rosh on Tamid, the Rambam on Kinnim and Middos) and the secondary commentaries, the Mesivta, and the delivered English of whole amudim, plus MDY's own Bechoros, which is labelled as not house style wherever it appears.
  Since 28 Oct 2026 (`ask-tools.js`, switched on by `askExtras` in `config.js` once the relay offers the tools in `relay-tools.json`) he can also find and read live files in the library (drafts, edited amudim, sheets) as the person asking, take attachments in the question box (Word, PDF, Excel, text, pictures), hand back a Word file with his edits as tracked changes and comments (downloaded on the device, never saved to SharePoint), and search the team chat log and the Proofreading and Graphics feedback sheets. To keep the cost down, files are read in parts of about 9,000 characters, an attachment goes with the question only as a short beginning, pictures are capped at two a question and dropped from the history after the answer, and the search results inside one question are held under 80,000 characters.

Settings (app registration, list ids) are in `config.js`. None of them are secrets.
